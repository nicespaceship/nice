Workspaces

A workspace is an orchestrator with a team of agents. It's the unit that runs a Process: every Run belongs to a workspace, even if the team is just one agent.

How Workspaces Work

  1. Activate a workspace from the catalog or build one in the Workshop
  2. Assign your team — drop agents into the workspace's team slots in the Schematic view
  3. Send a process — by default the workspace runs a triage step: an LLM router reads your prompt + the team roster and dispatches to the best-fit agent
  4. Agents collaborate via the Ship's Log — a shared conversation context every team member can read and append to

MCPs Mount at the Workspace Level

External integrations (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, custom MCP servers) are connected at the workspace level, not to individual agents. Every team member inherits the connections, so a single OAuth grant covers the whole team.

Crew Designer

Use the Crew Designer (Build an AI Team on the home screen) to describe your business in plain English. Longeron proposes a workspace configuration — picks a blueprint, fills the slots with agents whose roles match what you described, and lets you adjust before you deploy.
