01How it works

How a mission flies

Four steps from an idea to work you can send. You pick the crew and give the order; the crew does the work; you have the last word.

  1. 01

    Crew a spaceship

    A spaceship is your team: a lead agent and up to eleven specialists. Start from one built for a business like yours, then give each crew member the model that suits its job.

  2. 02

    Give it a mission

    Say what you need in plain words. NICE turns it into a plan: who does what, what runs side by side, and where it stops for you. Run it once or every morning.

  3. 03

    The crew works it

    Agents work through your real tools, share one log, and hand results down the line. A reviewer sends a draft back until it passes.

  4. 04

    You give the OK

    The result waits in your Outbox. Read it, edit it, approve or reject it. Approving a paused mission lets the crew finish the rest.

05The NICE Codex

NICE, explained

Every part of NICE, one entry at a time. Start at the top, or search for what you want to know.

What NICE is

NICE is mission control for AI agents. You put a crew of agents on a spaceship, give each one the AI model that suits its job, and hand the crew a mission. The agents split the work, pass results to each other and check each other's output.

By default, anything that leaves your business, like an email, a post or a changed file, waits for your OK. NICE stands for Neural Intelligence Command Engine, and NICE SPACESHIP makes it.

Your first spaceship

Open the Library and pick a spaceship built for a business like yours: a restaurant, a salon, a law firm, a software startup. Its crew comes staffed, each agent with a role, a model and the tools it works through.

Change anyone you like, connect the tools your crew needs, then send your first message from any crew card. Home lists your spaceships and anything that needs you.

Hosted or open source

At nicespaceship.ai, NICE SPACESHIP runs everything for you. Sign up with Google or email, and Gemini 2.5 Flash is free with no card and no API keys: NICE holds the keys for every provider, so you never paste one in.

The free plan runs one spaceship at a time. Pro brings in more models and lets you run several spaceships at once, and add-ons bring in Claude and the premium models.

The NICE app is open source under the MIT license, and its code is on GitHub.

Spaceships

A spaceship is a team of agents built around one business or one line of work. It has a lead agent and up to eleven stations for specialists, twelve crew in all.

Each spaceship keeps its own log, its own missions and its own run history, so a restaurant crew and a marketing crew never mix up their work.

Agents

An agent is one crew member: a role, its instructions, the AI model it thinks with, and the tools it may use. A bookkeeper might run on one model and read Stripe; a writer might run on another and save drafts to Google Drive.

Agents can have memory, so they learn from what you approve and what you turn down.

Crew cards

Each spaceship shows its crew as a table of cards. A card's frame takes the color of the lab behind its model, so you see at a glance who is thinking with what.

A card shows the agent's role, model and tools, whether it is working right now, its latest exchange, and a box to message it directly. A diagram view shows the same crew as a schematic.

The Library

The Library holds ready-made spaceships and agents, and the tools they connect to. Spaceships are built for real businesses: The Galley for a restaurant, The Chambers for a law firm, The Loft for a software startup, The Practice for a healthcare office, The Lobby for a boutique hotel, and more.

Build your own when nothing fits: describe your business and NICE drafts a crew, or start from an empty spaceship.

Missions

A mission is a job you hand a spaceship: what to do, which crew members do it, and in what order. Write it in plain words and NICE drafts the plan.

Run a mission once, or put it on a schedule, like every weekday at 7 a.m. in your time zone. Schedules run on NICE's servers, so your computer can stay off.

Steps and handoffs

Behind every mission is a plan of steps. Steps run one after another, or side by side and merge. A quality loop sends a draft back to the writer until a reviewer passes it. A triage step sends work to the right specialist, a condition takes one branch or another, and an approval step stops and waits for you.

A step can also wait, call a webhook, send a notice, or run one of NICE's own commands.

Runs

Each time a mission runs, NICE records the run: what each agent did, on which model, and what it made. A run moves from queued to running, then to review or done.

Cancel a run that's going the wrong way, retry one that failed, and look back at every run in the spaceship's history.

The spaceship's log

Agents on a spaceship share one log. The writer sees what the researcher found, and the reviewer sees both, so the work builds on itself instead of starting over.

The Outbox

Everything your crew makes lands in the Outbox. Results that need you wait under Needs you: read them, edit them, approve or reject them. Approving a paused mission lets the crew finish the rest.

Approved work is ready to copy or export, and a week view shows what goes out each day. The Outbox's count follows you in the sidebar and on Home.

Review mode

Review mode stops an agent before anything with side effects, like sending an email, booking a meeting or changing a file, and asks you first. You decide how much each spaceship may do on its own.

The model lineup

Claude Sonnet and Opus from Anthropic; GPT-5.4 Pro, GPT-5 mini and o3 from OpenAI; Gemini 2.5 Flash and Pro from Google; Grok from xAI; DeepSeek; Kimi from Moonshot AI; Nemotron from NVIDIA; and GPT-OSS on Groq.

Turn models on and off like channels, and give every agent the one that suits its job.

NICE Auto

In chat, NICE Auto picks a model for each message: a quick one for small talk, a stronger one for code, long documents or careful reasoning. It stays on the models your plan includes, so it never spends what you didn't choose to.

Space

Space is where you work with the models. Every model you turn on gets its own card, and the prompt sits at the bottom.

Click a card to add it to your next prompt or take it out. Ask one model or several: each one answers in its own card and keeps its own thread, so you can read the answers side by side.

Integrations

Agents work where your work lives: Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, Microsoft 365, Slack, GitHub, Notion, HubSpot, Linear, Jira and Confluence, Airtable, Klaviyo, Miro, Sentry, Stripe and Cloudflare.

Each connects with its own sign-in, so NICE never sees your password, and you can disconnect any of them at any time. They connect over MCP, the open standard for agent tools.

The web and files

Agents can read web pages and keep working files of their own. In chat you can attach images, PDFs, audio, video and text, and NICE moves you to a model that can read them when the one you picked can't.

Themes

Pick a look for your bridge: the clean NICE default, a red-eyed HAL-9000, The Grid, The Matrix, LCARS, J.A.R.V.I.S., Cyberpunk, RX-78-2 or 16-BIT. The Office trades the space words for office ones, so missions become assignments.
